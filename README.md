# Pixel Transparency Shader for RetroArch – Sunlight Shimmer Edition
_v2.0 by mattakins – Copyright (C) 2026 Matt Akins._

<img width="2240" height="1008" alt="image (28)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/226d963a-0a63-4959-a64f-4f16d45d55f4" />

Grid shaders look great on Game Boy Color games - except for one glaring issue.

With no backlight, the original devices didn't render white pixels. Light pixels appeared as transparent areas where the backing material of the screen showed through. Many games relied on this effect for transparent backgrounds that now render on modern displays as eye-searing white pixels. No more! Pixel Transparency simulates this unique effect.

**SUNLIGHT SHIMMER EDITION:** Light and shadows move with your device! Tilt your device and shadows shift with it. Glare sweeps across your screen. And just like the Game Boy Color in sunlight, you see a brilliant rainbow shimmer in the background.

Just append to your favorite LCD shader as the last pass to reduce eye strain and maximize nostalgia.
