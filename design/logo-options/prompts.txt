Pixel Reveal
Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished logo option for the existing project "Pixel Transparency", a RetroArch shader that restores reflective Game Boy Color LCD appearance: pale pixels reveal the screen backing, pixels cast subtle shadows, and sunlight creates a rainbow shimmer. Asset: standalone square logo concept PNG, real transparent background. Center a distinctive simple mark above the exact two-line wordmark "Pixel" / "Transparency". Typography should be exceptionally legible, restrained, thoughtfully spaced, and complement the mark. Large mark, clear silhouette, generous clear margins. Flat vector-like geometry, crisp edges, limited detail, suitable for later vector recreation. Use dark olive-charcoal ink and muted LCD green with any restrained iridescent accents justified by the concept. Deliver ONE logo only, no contact sheet, no mockup, no presentation frame, no labels, no tagline, no watermarks, no trademarked hardware outlines or existing brand logos. The checkerboard pattern must never be used as the background; preserve actual alpha transparency outside the artwork.
Specific direction: A disciplined 4-by-4 square LCD pixel matrix: dense solid green pixels at lower-left transition toward upper-right into outlined pixel cells and negative-space missing cells. One lifted corner pixel hints at the backing revealed underneath. Bold compact geometry and a calm modern sans-serif wordmark. Prioritize simplicity and recognizability.
